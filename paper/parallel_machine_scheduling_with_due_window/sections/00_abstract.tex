\begin{abstract}
We study an identical parallel-machine scheduling problem in which each job must
either be processed internally or outsourced. Internally processed jobs incur
sequence-dependent setup times and costs as well as job-dependent due-window
penalties. Outsourced jobs are charged through a nondecreasing concave
piecewise-linear tariff defined on their aggregate baseline outsourcing cost.
The resulting problem couples machine assignment, sequencing, timing, and outsourcing
decisions. We develop two set-covering column-generation formulations. The first represents
both internal schedules and outsourced subsets by columns, whereas the second keeps
the outsourcing decisions and tariff segments explicitly in the master problem.
Their common internal pricing problem is solved by a continuous-time bidirectional
labeling algorithm in which the timing cost of a partial sequence is represented by
a piecewise-linear function. An ng-route relaxation with decremental state-space
refinement provides an exact pricing certificate while controlling the label space.
We embed the formulations and pricing algorithms in a branch-and-price framework
with heuristic pricing, completion bounds, reduced-cost fixing, strong branching,
and Phase-I repair of restricted master problems. For integer-time instances, a
time-indexed pricing algorithm provides an exact alternative and a computational
reference for assessing sensitivity to the time horizon.
\end{abstract}
