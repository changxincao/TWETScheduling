This section will report the computational experiments after the instance-generation
protocol and final comparison profiles have been fixed. The study will distinguish
between (i) the internal-pricing comparison, which isolates the effect of the time
horizon and due-window width, and (ii) the comparison between the two outsourcing
formulations. No computational claim is made in the present draft.

\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This section will be completed after the computational study. The intended conclusion
will separate the modeling contribution---joint internal scheduling and aggregate
outsourcing---from the algorithmic contribution---a horizon-robust continuous-time
pricing method and an alternative time-indexed implementation.
